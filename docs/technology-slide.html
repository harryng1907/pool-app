The matching engine

An explainable recommendation system — works from the very first user, no training data needed.
1
Scores everyone who fits
Shared courses Shared hobbies Similar own words Said “I'm in” too Past ratings
2
Applies hard rules
Same squad-size preference, no reports, nobody rated ≤2★, actually free.
3
Solves the when & where
Earliest time everyone is free this week, at a public venue nobody rated badly.
4
Explains every match
“3 of you take COMP1511 · everyone is free Thu 2pm · you rated Mei 5★”.
5
Learns from feedback
Swipes re-rank the deck; ratings steer who and where you're matched next.

Why logic in the database

Matching, privacy and scheduling run as SQL functions next to the data — one source of truth, nothing secret on the phone.

Why a web app first

No app-store wait, works on any phone from a QR code, and the same code can ship to iOS / Android later.

Why explainable scoring

Every match says why — important for trust with shy students, and it needs no training data at launch.
